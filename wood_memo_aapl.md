# Apple Inc. (AAPL) | Cathie Wood–inspired investment memo

*Educational, investor-inspired interpretation.*

**Legend Panel verdict: Falls short of the growth screen.**

## Revenue growth and research intensity

Apple’s revenue grew at a compound annual rate of **4.97%** from the fiscal year ended September 24, 2022, through the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026. That fell below the panel’s **15%** threshold. The result measures growth over the panel’s historical window. It cannot establish how quickly revenue would grow if a new offering gained broad adoption.

In the fiscal year ended September 26, 2026, Apple’s R&D expense was **9.56%** of revenue, above the panel’s **5%** threshold. That shows how much Apple spent on reported R&D relative to sales. Future returns depend on whether that spending leads to offerings customers adopt and pay for, and whether those offerings generate enough incremental profit to justify the investment.

## Innovation thesis to test

One hypothesis is that a research-led offering could lower the cost of serving customers and make it economical to reach buyers Apple previously could not serve profitably. A milestone to look for would be sustained paid adoption among new customers after launch, alongside disclosed evidence that the cost to serve them fell. Customer-cohort data, revenue disclosures, and unit-cost or margin disclosures would help assess whether adoption is expanding Apple’s market and generating incremental profit.
